# Daily Level Suggestions

Every day, this mod picks a few **Demon List** levels for you to try.

- Press the green star button in the main menu's bottom bar.
- Your picks stay the same all day, so you can work through them.
- Beaten levels get a check mark.
- **Reroll** gives you a fresh set.
- **View** jumps straight to the level.

Customize how many levels, which list range, and whether beaten levels are skipped in the mod settings.

List data comes from [Pointercrate](https://pointercrate.com).
